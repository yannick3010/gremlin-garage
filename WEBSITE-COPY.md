### FAQ Section

**Do I need my own server?**
You can run OpenClaw on your Mac/PC, a VPS (like DigitalOcean or Hetzner), or a home server. If you don't have one, we can help you spin up a VPS for ~$5-10/month.

**Is my data safe?**
We use secure access methods (SSH keys, screen sharing) and never store your credentials. You revoke our access the moment we're done. We can also work entirely via screen share if you prefer.

**What messaging apps are supported?**
Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage, Signal, and more. We'll set up whichever ones you use.

**What are "skills"?**
Skills are capabilities you give to OpenClaw — like checking your calendar, searching the web, controlling your smart home, or running custom automations. Pro/Premium packages include custom skills tailored to your workflow.

**Can I add more skills later?**
Yes! We offer skill development as an add-on service, or you can build your own using OpenClaw's skill framework.

**What if something breaks?**
Pro and Premium packages include 30-day support. After that, you can add our monthly maintenance plan ($99/mo) for ongoing support and updates.

**What if I need something more custom?**
That's our specialty. If you need a purpose-built AI agent or complex automation, [book a strategy call](/book) and we'll scope a custom project.
